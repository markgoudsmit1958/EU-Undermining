Lobby Dashboard

Who is shaping this law?

Fourteen EU legislative files, opened up: who lobbies, how much they spend, which MEPs they meet — and what ended up in the final text.

EU institutions decide. But citizens rarely see who was in the room. The data exists — scattered across registers, meeting logs and procedure files. This dashboard puts it back together, one dossier at a time.

One example of fourteen — EU climate framework
Annual EU climate-lobbying spend, 2019
Heavy industry & fossil-fuel coalition €43.5m
Paris-aligned "green" coalition €2.4m

An 18-to-1 spending gap. Industry lobbied hard against the EU's proposed 2040 climate target — a 90% cut in emissions versus 1990 levels — calling it unachievable. The target was adopted anyway, at exactly the level the Commission first proposed. Money buys access; it does not decide outcomes. Separating those two is what this dashboard does.

Spending figures: Climate Social Science Network, 2019 reference year. Each of the fourteen dossiers has its own page, with full sourcing and stated data gaps.
