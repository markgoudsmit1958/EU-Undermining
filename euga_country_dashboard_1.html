EU Governance Assessment — country dashboard

This dashboard translates the EU Governance Assessment (EUGA) index into a navigable, sourced tool for tracking how EU member states measure up against the values set out in Article 2 TEU. Each of the 26 member states with sufficient source data has a complete dossier — trend view, component breakdown, qualitative context and sourcing. The full methodology, including indicator selection, weighting and the signal model, is set out in the accompanying methodology memorandum.

SourceNatureCertainty
Certainty rubric: High = primary legal/institutional record (laws, court and tribunal rulings, official EU/UN/government documents, official inquiry reports) or a flagship, methodologically transparent comparative index/monitor (V-Dem, Freedom House, WJP, BTI, EUI-affiliated monitors). Medium = news reporting (including investigative journalism), NGO/advocacy statements and reports, think-tank or policy-institute analysis, non-peer-reviewed academic commentary, and reference works. This distinguishes primary/index-grade sources from credible but secondary or interpretive ones; it is not a judgement on accuracy.
